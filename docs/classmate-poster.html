ClassMate gives students a chatbot that actually knows their course — grounded in their own lecture material, not the general web. Ask about any topic and get a detailed answer, explained in the course's own terms and linked back to the moment it was taught. Under the hood, it turns raw lecture videos into a searchable knowledge base — transcribing and indexing every lecture — then orchestrates a cascade of LLMs that routes each question, pulls the right passages through RAG with hybrid (lexical + semantic) retrieval, and answers from what was actually said in class.
